# Project 1 — Research Artifact Status

**Execution date:** 2026-09-13

## Artifact

**Title:** Graph-Based Agricultural Supply Network Optimization for Emerging Economies

## Research question

How can a constrained network-flow optimization model allocate limited agricultural supply to multiple markets while minimizing transport cost and quantifying unmet demand under supply, demand and transport shocks?

## Contribution

A transparent computational framework combining:
- graph/network representation;
- linear programming;
- corridor capacity constraints;
- explicit unmet-demand variables;
- controlled sensitivity analysis;
- provenance separation between public reference data and synthetic experimental inputs.

## Evidence classes

1. Public FAO/GIEWS reference evidence.
2. Locked FAOSTAT source definitions.
3. Synthetic computational scenarios.

The third class is explicitly synthetic and cannot be described as field observations.

## Executed evidence

- Phase 20 baseline + four shock scenarios solved.
- Phase 21 optimal flow matrices documented.
- Phase 22 sensitivity analysis executed across demand, supply and transport-cost shocks.

## Remaining research gap

Market-level empirical validation, calibrated transport costs, observed market demand and observed corridor flows remain to be collected.
