# Phase 22 — Sensitivity Analysis

## Executed experiment

Sensitivity was run over:
- demand shocks: 0%, +10%, +20%, +30%, +40%;
- supply shocks: 0%, -10%, -20%, -30%, -40%;
- transport-cost shocks: 0%, +15%, +30%, +45%, +60%.

All inputs are SYNTHETIC.

## Main findings

Demand and supply shocks change feasibility and unmet demand. Transport-cost shocks alone preserve the same feasible quantities in this network and scale transport cost proportionally.

The machine-readable results are in `BOI_Project1_Phase22_Sensitivity.xlsx`.

## Interpretation boundary

The sensitivity analysis establishes robustness/response of the mathematical formulation under controlled assumptions. It is not evidence of actual Burundian market elasticities.
