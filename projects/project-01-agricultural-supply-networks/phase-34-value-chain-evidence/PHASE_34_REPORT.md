# Phase 34 — Value Chain Evidence

Search completed 2026-09-13.

A Burundi maize value-chain study reports intermediaries sourcing 86% from producers and 10% from farmer cooperatives; 43% of intermediaries were traders, 30% processors, 11% retailers, 7% assemblers, 6% collectors, 2% wholesalers and 1% transporters. It also reports 55% of maize production sold and 45% self-consumed, with most sold production directed to local markets. These are study-level structural observations, not transaction-level corridor margins.

A 2025 Burundi government meeting on maize commercialization brought together processors and wholesale grain traders and explicitly targeted coordination among producers, processors and traders.

The evidence still does not provide one linked origin-quantity-purchase-transport-destination-sale transaction. Therefore no empirical net-margin coefficient is admitted.

Status: value-chain structure improved; transaction linkage not passed; full empirical validation not passed.
