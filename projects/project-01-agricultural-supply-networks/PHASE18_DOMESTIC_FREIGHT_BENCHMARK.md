# Phase 18 — Domestic Freight Benchmark

## Execution date
2026-09-12

## New documented observation
Burundi Eco reported a trader's 2024 experience transporting 200 kg of maize from Kayanza to Bujumbura for 60,000 FBu during a fuel shortage.

Source: Burundi Eco, “Quand la pénurie du carburant handicape le transport des marchandises”.

## External route metadata
A road-distance source reports approximately 104 km from Kayanza to Bujumbura. This distance is external route metadata and is not presented as part of the original freight observation.

## Derived benchmark
Load = 0.2 tonne
Freight payment = 60,000 BIF
External road distance = 104 km
Derived freight rate = 60,000 / (0.2 × 104) = 2,884.6 BIF/tonne-km.

## Interpretation
This is the first documented domestic agricultural freight observation in the BOI intake with product, payload, origin, destination and freight payment. However, it occurred during a documented fuel shortage and therefore should not be treated as a representative central tariff.

It is retained as an empirical benchmark and stress/outlier observation. It must not by itself set the Phase 15 central parameter.

## Calibration status
Calibration remains BLOCKED because one domestic observation is insufficient to estimate a stable central value, lower/upper quantiles, seasonal variation, road-condition sensitivity, or out-of-sample performance.

## Next target
Acquire multiple domestic observations across products, routes, seasons and transport modes, preferably with original route distance and trip-level documentation.
