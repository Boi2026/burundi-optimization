# Phase 16 QA Checklist

- [ ] Every row has a unique observation_id.
- [ ] Observation date is valid and within the declared collection period.
- [ ] Source and collection method are documented.
- [ ] Units are explicit and internally consistent.
- [ ] Currency is explicit for monetary observations.
- [ ] Raw values are preserved.
- [ ] Standardization formulas are documented.
- [ ] BOI market matching is recorded; unmatched records are retained.
- [ ] Freight records have valid positive load and distance before BIF/tonne-km is calculated.
- [ ] Duplicate or contradictory records are flagged.
- [ ] No synthetic observations are introduced.
- [ ] QA results and dataset version are recorded in the manifest.
- [ ] Calibration and validation samples are separated where feasible.
