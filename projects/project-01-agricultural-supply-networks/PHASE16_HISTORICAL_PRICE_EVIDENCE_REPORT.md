# Phase 16 — Historical Price Evidence Acquisition Report

## Execution status
**PASS — 30 documented historical price observations acquired and provenance-preserved.**

## Source
FAO/WFP, *Crop and Food Supply Assessment Mission to Burundi*, 4 March 1997, Table 4:
https://www.fao.org/4/W4490e/W4490e00.htm

The source states that the table combines official ISTEBU survey data for Bujumbura with Ministry of Agriculture price information and direct observations in rural markets. The table reports consumer prices in FBU/kg.

## Coverage
- 30 observations
- 6 BOI priority market nodes: Rutana, Makamba, Bururi, Muramvya, Gitega, Cankuzo
- 3 products: beans, maize, rice
- 2 periods: July 1996 and January 1997
- Unit: FBU/kg
- Currency standardized to BIF because the historical Burundi franc is the currency unit reported in the source
- Market matching: standardized against BOI priority market-node names

## Scientific limitation
These observations are **historical evidence**, not current transport observations. They are stored separately from the live empirical collection table and must **not** be used as evidence that current 2026 transport parameters are empirically validated.

They may be used for:
- provenance and pipeline testing;
- historical descriptive comparison;
- testing market/product matching;
- demonstrating the Phase 16 intake workflow.

They must not be used to infer current freight tariffs from price gaps.

## Gate
The Phase 19 empirical freeze remains blocked until sufficient current/relevant external observations are acquired and quality-checked.
