# Phase 15 Data Dictionary

## Core concepts

**Observed price** — external transaction/market price used for validation.

**Observed freight cost** — empirical transport cost associated with a shipment or route.

**BIF/tonne-km** — standardized freight-cost unit:
freight cost in BIF divided by transported tonnes and road distance in km.

**Calibration sample** — observations used to estimate or select model parameters.

**Validation sample** — observations withheld from calibration and used to evaluate model performance.

**MAE** — mean absolute error.

**RMSE** — root mean squared error.

**MAPE** — mean absolute percentage error; should not be used where observed values are zero or otherwise unsuitable.

**Bias** — mean signed prediction error.

**Recalibrated corridor** — corridor ranking obtained after replacing illustrative transport assumptions with empirically supported parameters.
