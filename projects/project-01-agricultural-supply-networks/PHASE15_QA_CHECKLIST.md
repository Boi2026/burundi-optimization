# Phase 15 — QA Checklist

- [ ] All source observations have provenance.
- [ ] Units are standardized.
- [ ] Dates are valid and documented.
- [ ] Product names are harmonized.
- [ ] Market identifiers are matched to BOI identifiers.
- [ ] Duplicate observations are reviewed.
- [ ] Missing values are quantified.
- [ ] Outliers are flagged and not silently removed.
- [ ] Freight costs are converted to comparable BIF/tonne-km units.
- [ ] Distance definition is documented.
- [ ] Calibration and validation samples are separated where feasible.
- [ ] Model parameters are versioned.
- [ ] Recalibrated results are reproducible.
- [ ] No empirical validation claim is made without sufficient observations.
