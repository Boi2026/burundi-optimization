# BOI Institutional Profile

## Organization

**Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI)**

**Positioning:** Research-focused computational methods for complex problems in Burundi and emerging economies.

## Research scope

BOI develops computational research across:

- combinatorial and algorithmic optimization
- operations research
- artificial intelligence and machine learning
- data science and analytics
- graph theory and network analysis
- spatial intelligence
- logistics and resource allocation
- agricultural supply networks
- computational modelling

## Public research infrastructure

BOI maintains a public research record through:

- **Official website:** https://burundioptimization.com
- **Public GitHub repository:** https://github.com/Boi2026/burundi-optimization
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/burundi-optimization-intelligence-boi/
- **Flagship research record:** https://burundioptimization.com/research/project-1

The website and repository are complementary: the website presents the research programme and evidence architecture, while the repository preserves technical records, code, methodology, research phases and reproducibility materials.

## Flagship research

**Agricultural Supply Network Optimization in Burundi**

A graph-based combinatorial optimization research programme studying market connectivity, transport sensitivity, corridor prioritization and multi-product allocation.

## Scientific status

Project 1 is maintained as a reproducible analytical research record. Its empirical validation remains incomplete and is not represented as complete validation.

BOI distinguishes:

1. computational modelling;
2. analytical results;
3. public and secondary evidence;
4. empirical observations;
5. validation;
6. future recalibration.

## Evidence standard

BOI does not represent synthetic, estimated or derived values as field observations. Important research inputs should retain provenance, date, geographic reference, unit and methodological status where available.

## External presentation

When BOI is presented to external research organizations or conferences, the organization should be described through verifiable public records rather than unsupported claims of recognition, endorsement, publication, partnership or validation.

## Collaboration

BOI is open to research, data and technology collaboration with researchers, universities, research groups, technology organizations, development practitioners and data partners.
