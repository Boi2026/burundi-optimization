About BOI

Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI) develops and explores computational and data-driven approaches to complex problems. Our work connects mathematical and computational methods with practical applications.

Our Mission

To investigate how algorithms, artificial intelligence, optimization and data analysis can support better decision-making, efficient resource allocation and technological innovation.

Areas of Focus

BOI focuses on computational approaches that can be adapted to real-world problems in developing and emerging markets.

Artificial Intelligence

Exploring AI and machine-learning approaches for data-driven decision-making and practical applications.

Algorithms & Optimization

Studying algorithmic and optimization methods for complex problems involving resources, networks and decision-making.

Data Science

Using data analysis and computational techniques to extract useful insights and support evidence-based decisions.

Applied Research

Connecting computational research with practical technological challenges in emerging economies.

Application Domains

Complex Networks

Exploring graph-based modelling and network analysis for interconnected systems.

Logistics

Investigating optimization approaches for transportation, routing and resource allocation.

Agricultural Systems

Exploring data-driven and optimization approaches for agricultural supply chains and resource management.

Emerging Economies

Adapting computational methods to contexts where resources, infrastructure and data availability present unique challenges.

Research & Development

BOI is developing its research and technology portfolio. This section will document research projects, technical work, publications and collaborations as they become available.

Current Direction

Algorithms, artificial intelligence, graph-based modelling, combinatorial optimization, data analysis and applications to logistics and agricultural systems.

Research Collaboration

BOI is interested in connecting with researchers, universities, technology organizations and practitioners working on algorithms, discrete mathematics, optimization, AI and data science.
