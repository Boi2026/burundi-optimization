About BOI

A research-driven technology initiative

Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI) develops and explores computational and data-driven approaches to complex problems, connecting mathematical and computational methods with practical applications.

Mission

To investigate how algorithms, artificial intelligence, optimization and data analysis can support better decisions, efficient resource allocation and technological innovation.

Vision

To contribute to a stronger ecosystem of computational research and responsible technology in Burundi and across emerging economies.

Research Philosophy

We combine mathematical reasoning, computational experimentation and real-world problem formulation to move from abstract methods toward useful applications.

Context

BOI pays particular attention to environments where data, infrastructure and financial or computational resources may be limited.

Research

Core research areas

BOI focuses on computational disciplines that can be adapted to complex real-world systems.

01

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning and AI approaches for prediction, classification, decision support and practical data-driven applications.

02

Algorithms & Optimization

Algorithmic and optimization methods for resource allocation, scheduling, routing and difficult combinatorial problems.

03

Data Science

Data analysis, statistical reasoning and computational techniques for extracting useful and actionable insights.

04

Graph Theory & Networks

Graph-based modelling and network analysis for interconnected systems, infrastructure and complex relationships.

05

Mathematical Modelling

Mathematical representations of real systems to understand, simulate and optimize decisions under constraints.

06

Operations Research

Quantitative methods for planning, logistics, decision-making and efficient use of scarce resources.

Applications

Data-driven approaches for agricultural planning, supply chains, resource management and production systems.

Logistics & Transportation

Routing, scheduling, network analysis and optimization for transportation and distribution systems.

Computational methods for allocating limited resources across competing needs and operational constraints.

Emerging Economies

Methods designed with real constraints in mind, including limited infrastructure, incomplete data and constrained resources.
