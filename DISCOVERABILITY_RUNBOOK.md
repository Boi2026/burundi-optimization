# BOI Discoverability Runbook

## Objective

Make BOI independently discoverable without relying on a single conference, email exchange or private explanation.

## Search/indexing checks

- Verify official domain resolves over HTTPS.
- Verify robots.txt is accessible.
- Verify sitemap.xml is accessible.
- Submit sitemap to Google Search Console.
- Request indexing for:
  - /
  - /about
  - /research
  - /research/project-1
  - /evidence
- Verify canonical tags.
- Verify unique page titles and descriptions.
- Verify Open Graph metadata.
- Verify structured organization metadata where appropriate.

## Identity consistency

Use exactly:
**Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI)**

Core positioning:
**Research-focused computational methods for complex problems in Burundi and emerging economies.**

Keep website, GitHub, LinkedIn and presentation materials synchronized.

## Evidence expansion

Prioritize dated, independently inspectable outputs:
- research records;
- technical notes;
- code releases;
- talks;
- collaboration pages;
- finished datasets;
- finished papers.

## Measurement

Maintain a dated log of:
- indexed pages;
- external mentions;
- inbound links;
- research downloads;
- collaborations;
- completed validation gates.

## Status rule

Do not claim "widely recognized", "peer-reviewed", "published", "endorsed", "partnered" or "empirically validated" unless the public evidence supports the claim.
