# BOI Public Credibility & Presentation Audit

Date: 2026-09-12

## Objective

The purpose of this document is to make BOI independently understandable and verifiable as a research-focused organization. It is not a conference-submission document.

## Evidence currently available

1. Public website: https://burundioptimization.com
2. Public GitHub repository: https://github.com/Boi2026/burundi-optimization
3. Public research record for Project 1.
4. Versioned research phases and analytical outputs in the repository.
5. Public methodology, data-source, validation, reproducibility and citation sections.
6. Public research portfolio covering optimization, AI, data science, graph/network methods and spatial intelligence.

## What the public record demonstrates

BOI has a public research platform and a versioned computational research record. Project 1 is documented as an analytical/reproducible research record.

## What the public record must NOT imply

The current public record must not imply that Project 1 has completed empirical validation when the repository explicitly states that empirical validation and transport recalibration remain incomplete.

Synthetic or estimated values must not be represented as observed field measurements.

BOI must not claim algorithmic novelty unless a prior-art review and appropriate technical evidence support the claim.

## Presentation principle

The strongest presentation is evidence-led:

BOI exists -> BOI publishes -> BOI documents methods -> BOI exposes research phases -> BOI reports limitations -> BOI builds toward empirical validation.

## Priority actions

- Keep organization identity consistent across website, GitHub and professional profiles.
- Keep the flagship project clearly labelled as an analytical/reproducible record until empirical gates pass.
- Add public evidence navigation so a third party can verify the research record without contacting BOI.
- Maintain dated/versioned records for major research milestones.
- Add institutional contact and collaboration information.
