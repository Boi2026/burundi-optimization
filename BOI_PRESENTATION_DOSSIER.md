# Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI)
## Public Presentation Dossier

### 1. Identity

**Name:** Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI)

**Positioning:** Research-focused computational methods for complex problems in Burundi and emerging economies.

### 2. What BOI does

BOI develops computational research across:
- algorithmic and combinatorial optimization;
- operations research;
- artificial intelligence and machine learning;
- data science and analytics;
- graph theory and complex networks;
- spatial intelligence;
- logistics and resource allocation;
- agricultural supply networks;
- computational modelling.

### 3. Flagship research

**Agricultural Supply Network Optimization in Burundi**

A graph-based computational research programme studying market connectivity, transport sensitivity, corridor prioritization and multi-product allocation.

Public research record:
https://burundioptimization.com/research/project-1

### 4. Why the project matters

The project uses mathematical and computational methods to study complex network and allocation problems relevant to agricultural markets and transport systems.
