# BOI Research Roadmap 2026–2027

## Strategic objective

Build BOI into a credible, independently verifiable research and technology organization through substantive outputs rather than promotional claims.

## Phase 1 — Public foundation
Status: substantially complete.
- Organization identity
- Website
- GitHub
- Research portfolio
- Evidence map
- Credibility audit
- Presentation assets

## Phase 2 — Research output
Status: initiated.
- Research Output 01 published as a technical research note.
- Research outputs index created.
- Scientific status labels established.

## Phase 3 — Reproducible experiment
Target:
- Freeze a public dataset/version.
- Create machine-readable parameter file.
- Fix random seed where stochastic methods are used.
- Publish executable experiment instructions.
- Produce machine-readable output tables.
- Produce figures directly from code.
- Record software/environment versions.

## Phase 4 — Empirical validation
Target:
- Identify authoritative or field data.
- Document provenance and access date.
- Compare model assumptions against observations.
- Quantify error/sensitivity where appropriate.
- Record failed validation checks as well as successful ones.
- Recalibrate only when evidence supports recalibration.

## Phase 5 — External research footprint
Target:
- Persistent identifier for finished outputs where appropriate.
- Public talks/workshops.
- Research collaborations.
- Independent references/citations.
- Academic or technical feedback.

## Phase 6 — Institutional maturity
Target:
- Formal research governance.
- Versioned publication policy.
- Contributor/authorship policy.
- Data ethics/privacy policy.
- Conflict-of-interest policy.
- Research archive.
- Annual research report.

## Phase gate

No phase is marked complete merely because a document exists. A phase is complete only when its stated evidence exists and can be independently inspected.
