# Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI)

**Research-focused computational methods for complex problems in Burundi and emerging economies.**

Burundi Optimization & Intelligence (BOI) is a research-focused organization developing computational methods for complex problems involving optimization, artificial intelligence, data science, graph-based modelling, spatial intelligence, and operations research.

## Public Research Platform

**Website:** https://burundioptimization.com

The BOI platform provides a public, traceable record of research methods, analytical results, data sources, validation procedures, reproducibility materials, and research limitations.

## Research 01 — Flagship

### Agricultural Supply Network Optimization in Burundi

*A Graph-Based Combinatorial Optimization Framework for Market Connectivity, Transport Sensitivity, Corridor Prioritization, and Multi-Product Allocation*

Research 01 develops a graph-based combinatorial optimization framework for analysing agricultural market connectivity, transport sensitivity, corridor prioritization, and multi-product allocation in Burundi.

The research record documents:

- analytical methodology
- graph construction
- market and corridor modelling
- transport sensitivity analysis
- multi-product allocation

## Research Principles

BOI follows four core principles.

### 1. Evidence Provenance

Analytical inputs should have a documented source, provenance, or clearly stated derivation.

### 2. Reproducibility

Methods, assumptions, analytical steps, and research records should be sufficiently documented to allow independent examination and reproduction.

### 3. Explicit Limitations

Analytical results are distinguished from empirical validation. Where evidence is incomplete, the limitation is explicitly reported rather than concealed.

### 4. Iterative Research

Research is maintained as a versioned record. Methods, evidence, and results can be refined as new data and validation opportunities become available.

## Scientific Status

Research 01 is analytically finalized and documented as a reproducible research record.

Its empirical validation is explicitly incomplete. The analytical baseline should therefore not be interpreted as fully empirically validated.

BOI maintains this distinction between:

- computational modelling,
- analytical results,
- empirical evidence,
- validation,
- and future recalibration.

## Data and Evidence

BOI research may integrate publicly available datasets, geospatial information, statistical publications, market information, and other documented sources.

Where primary empirical observations are unavailable or insufficient, BOI does not fabricate observations or present synthetic values as measured evidence.
